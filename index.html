Install

Install from inside Cozy Studio.

You do not download anything from this site.

  1. 1

    Start Cozy Studio

    Essentials installs by itself the first time.

  2. 2

    Open Settings → Styles & workflows

    Under Get more packs, open a pack to see its cover and samples, then select Install.

  3. 3

    Reload Studio

    The new styles show in Create → Catalog. Mix up to five in one image.

Good to know

Questions about packs.

01 Do packs cost anything?

No. The packs and Cozy Studio are free. Making images still goes through your own provider account and its limits.

02 What are full-quality cards?

Each pack installs with small thumbnails. Tick Full-quality cards when you install a pack to also get its large sample images.

03 How do updates work?

When a pack gets a new version, Studio offers the update in Settings → Styles & workflows. It checks every download before it replaces the installed pack, and keeps the full cards you already have.

04 Why do some Essentials styles disappear?

Essentials copies its styles from the full packs. When you install a full pack, Studio hides the matching copies so a style never shows twice.

05 Can I ask for a style or report a problem?

Yes. Open an issue in the Cozy Studio repository on GitHub.
